Understanding Temple Sevas

Sevas are sacred offerings and rituals performed at the temple, allowing devotees to receive divine blessings and grace.

Each seva has a specific spiritual significance and purpose, from simple archanas to elaborate ceremonies. By participating in these sacred rituals, devotees can express their devotion, seek blessings, and contribute to the temple's ongoing services.

After booking your seva, you'll receive confirmation details and instructions. On your selected date, our priests will perform the ritual with utmost devotion and sanctity.
