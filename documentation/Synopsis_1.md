### Synopsis for the Project: Temple Management System

#### Title: Temple Management System

#### Objective of the Project

The goal of this project is to create a simple and user-friendly website for temples. This website will help manage all temple activities including user accounts, donations, sevas (religious services), events, and testimonials. It will make things easier for both temple staff and visitors.

#### Problem Statement

Today, most temples use paper records and manual systems. This creates many problems:

- Paper records can get lost or damaged
- People must visit the temple in person for most services
- Donation tracking is difficult and error-prone
- Event information is not easily available to everyone
- Staff spend too much time on paperwork instead of religious duties

#### Solution

Our Temple Management System solves these problems by providing an online platform that:

- Replaces paper records with a secure digital database
- Allows devotees to access temple services from anywhere
- Enables online donations with automatic receipts
- Provides up-to-date information about temple events and sevas
- Makes temple management tasks simpler and more efficient

The system uses modern web technologies like Python, Flask, and MongoDB to create a solution that is both powerful and easy to use. It respects temple traditions while bringing the benefits of digital technology.

#### Project Scope

The project currently focuses on a single temple and its community of devotees. It includes the core features needed for temple operations: user accounts, donation processing, seva bookings, event management, and testimonials.

In the future, the system can be expanded to:
- Support multiple temples under one management
- Offer additional services like prasadam delivery
- Provide more detailed analytics for temple administrators
- Include multi-language support for diverse communities

#### Expected Results

The Temple Management System will:
- Reduce paperwork and manual record keeping
- Make temple services available 24/7
- Improve financial tracking and transparency
- Increase community participation in temple events
- Allow staff to focus more on religious duties instead of administration

#### End Users

The system is designed to serve:
- Temple Managers and Staff
- Visitors and Worshippers
- Event Participants
- Donors and Sponsors
- Religious Service Participants
